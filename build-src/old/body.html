Your hotel is always moving.
Your workforce decisions should too.


Real-time workforce orchestration for hospitality. Here's the problem it solves, and how.







The problem
Your systems record the work. Your team still connects the dots.
The PMS knows which rooms need attention. Attendance knows who arrived. Task tools hold the list. Nothing decides who should act next, so a supervisor does it by radio and WhatsApp.

The problem
Then the day changes.
Hotel operations are event-driven. Workforce allocation usually isn't. Every change lands on the same few people.




Demand changesEarly arrival for Room 512 at 12:30Priorities shift from the morning plan.


Availability changesDaniel is running 25 minutes lateAbsences, breaks and workload change what the team can deliver.

The cost

The solution
Allocivo's agents connect the signals and decide the next move.
They sit above the systems you already run, combine demand, availability and your operating rules, and decide who should do what next.

How it works
Sense. Understand. Decide. Adapt.
A continuous loop, with people in control at every step.




1 SenseBring in live signalsRoom status, arrivals, attendance and task status, from systems you already use.


2 UnderstandRead the situationWorkload, skills, location, deadlines and dependencies.


3 DecideRecommend the next actionWho should do what next, using your rules.


4 AdaptRecalculate on changeWhen reality moves, the plan moves with it.

    The problem
    Every scanner hands you a list. Nobody hands you the fix.
    Reports say something may be wrong. Then the real work starts: understanding it, fixing it in the right place, and checking it worked.
    

    Typical scan report (illustrative)

    Name, role, value12 instances

    Who fixes all of this, and when?
    

    

    


    

    The problem
    Fixing is the slow part.
    

    

    Reports pile upFindings without actionHundreds of rule violations, little sense of what to change first.
    

    Fixes waitAccessibility sits in the backlogEvery fix competes with feature work for the same developers.
    

    Nobody re-checks"Done" is rarely verifiedChanges ship without anyone confirming they worked, or broke something else.
    

    

    


    

    The cost
    One finding, passed between four people.
    Each handoff loses time. The person using a screen reader or a keyboard waits for all of it.
    

    

    Product leadIs this urgent, or can it wait?
    

    DesignerWhat should it look like instead?
    

    DeveloperWhich element? When do I fit it in?
    

    EveryoneDid the fix actually work?

    Frequently asked
    Straight answers.
    

    

    Does Xessly really fix issues without people?
    Yes, for every fix its agents can verify. If an agent can't prove a fix, it flags the issue with a ready draft instead of guessing.
    

    Is it an overlay?
    No. Xessly changes your actual code and content through pull requests and CMS updates.
    

    What if a fix breaks something?
    The test agent checks every change for regressions before it counts, and every change rolls back in one click.
    

    Does it guarantee WCAG or legal compliance?
    No tool can honestly guarantee that. Xessly gives you a verified record of every fix; pair it with periodic testing by disabled users and legal advice for your situation.
