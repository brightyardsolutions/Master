  1. Allocivo · by Brightyard

    Your hotel is always moving.
    Your workforce decisions should too.

    Real-time workforce orchestration for hospitality. Here's the problem it solves, and how.

  2. The problem

    Your systems record the work. Your team still connects the dots.

    The PMS knows which rooms need attention. Attendance knows who arrived. Task tools hold the list. Nothing decides who should act next, so a supervisor does it by radio and WhatsApp.

    PMSRoom 418Ready by 14:00
    AttendanceDanielOn shift
    Task toolT-104Assigned to Daniel
    no shared decision
    Supervisor, floor 4Who's free on 4?
    Is 418 done yet?
    Front desk says 512 is here early
  3. The problem

    Then the day changes.

    Hotel operations are event-driven. Workforce allocation usually isn't. Every change lands on the same few people.

    Demand changesEarly arrival for Room 512 at 12:30Priorities shift from the morning plan.
    Availability changesDaniel is running 25 minutes lateAbsences, breaks and workload change what the team can deliver.
    Work gets disconnectedRoom 206 is blocked by maintenanceHousekeeping can't finish until the repair is closed.
  4. The cost

    Risk shows up when the deadline is already close.

    Without a live view, nobody sees that Room 418 will miss its target until a guest is waiting at the desk.

    12:30Early arrival for 512
    13:05Daniel running late
    13:40Supervisor finds out 418 isn't started
    14:00Room 418 target missed
  5. The solution

    Allocivo's agents connect the signals and decide the next move.

    They sit above the systems you already run, combine demand, availability and your operating rules, and decide who should do what next.

    Demand: arrivals, room status
    Availability: who's on shift
    Context: skills, workload, rules
    RecommendationMove Room 418 to AshaFree now, qualified for room turns, already on floor 4.
  6. How it works

    Sense. Understand. Decide. Adapt.

    A continuous loop, with people in control at every step.

    1 SenseBring in live signalsRoom status, arrivals, attendance and task status, from systems you already use.
    2 UnderstandRead the situationWorkload, skills, location, deadlines and dependencies.
    3 DecideRecommend the next actionWho should do what next, using your rules.
    4 AdaptRecalculate on changeWhen reality moves, the plan moves with it.
  7. In action

    When the plan breaks, the next move is already on screen.

    Daniel is late. Room 418 turns at risk. Allocivo proposes Asha, the supervisor approves, and the queue settles.

  8. Autonomy you set

    Agents act where you allow them. Managers handle the exceptions.

    Low-risk work moves automatically. Bigger changes wait for approval. Every action shows its reason.
